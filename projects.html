Cognitive Health · Digital Product

Active development

Mineuri

A digital cognitive health platform combining neuropsychological knowledge, structured cognitive activities, clinical review and product architecture.

The project explores how cognitive intervention can be transformed into a scalable digital system without losing clinical coherence, traceability or human oversight.

Neuropsychology · Cognitive Health

Clinical project

Dememoria

A neuropsychology and cognitive health project focused on cognitive assessment, dementia, intervention, emotional care and clinically meaningful support.

Dememoria represents the clinical foundation of my work and connects neuropsychological practice with education, digital resources and future technology-oriented projects.
