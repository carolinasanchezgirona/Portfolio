Brain Health Data Experiments

A growing collection of small computational experiments exploring biomedical signals, data visualisation, Python and responsible artificial intelligence.

Status Planned and in development
Focus Biomedical data and scientific computing
Tools Python, data visualisation and signal analysis

01

Project overview

This project documents my first computational explorations at the intersection of biomedical engineering, neuroscience and data science.

The aim is not simply to produce visualisations or code, but to understand how biological and clinical information can be represented, analysed and interpreted responsibly.

Each experiment will include the original question, the dataset or signal used, the analytical process, the limitations of the approach and a reflection on its possible relevance to brain health.

02

Areas of exploration

  • Visualisation of physiological and biomedical signals
  • Basic processing of heart-rate and pulse data
  • Introduction to EEG and neurological signal concepts
  • Exploratory analysis of cognitive and clinical datasets
  • Python workflows for scientific computing
  • Responsible and explainable artificial intelligence
